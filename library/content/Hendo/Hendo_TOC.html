Henderson's Tutor for the Bagpipe and Collection of Pipe Music

                       

Frontpiece from Henderson's Collection

Peter Henderson was a Glasgow bagpipe maker and published of bagpipe music.
His "Tutor for the Bagpipe and Collection of Pipe Music" was first published in 1900. A second edition of this work was published sometime between 1918 and 1932, and that second edition is presented here.
