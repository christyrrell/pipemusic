A New and Complete Tutor for the Great Highland Bagpipe by Thomas Glen

                       

East Princes Street Gardens, Edinburgh

Thomas Glen was a younger brother to Alexander Glen, and father of John and Robert Glen. He was born in 1804 and became a pipe maker in 1833.
His collection has two books, which were first published sometime between 1840 and 1843. There was a second edition, which was probably published in 1850 or earlier. The version presented here is from the second edition.
