To our Dear Friends and Followers

Ceol Sean is no longer offering the sale of its collections on CD. Over the past few years, sales demand for our CD’s has fallen to point such that it is no longer feasible for us to maintain our CD production capability.

We expected the discontinuation of CD sales to occur at some point after we began offering all of collections on-line for free in 2011. Since we began offering our collection for free on-line, the number of site visits has grown steadily each year, and we are pleased that pipers all over the world visit our site every day.

As in the past, we will be glad to work with piping researchers and historians who may need special access to our settings and collections or require copies of tune settings on digital media. Please contact uswith any special requests you might have. Thanks for your interest and support.

 

Steve Scaife
Ceol Sean
October 2017
Springfield, IL USA
