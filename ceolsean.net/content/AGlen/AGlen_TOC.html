The Caledonian Repository of Music for the Great Highland Bagpipe

                       

Frontpiece from Alexander Glen's Caledonian Repository

Alexander Glen was an older brother to Thomas Glen, and father of David Glen. He was born in 1801, and went into business as a bagpipe maker in 1844.
His "Caledonian Repository" was first published in 1860, and there were three other editions published over the next several years. The version presented here is a first edition.
