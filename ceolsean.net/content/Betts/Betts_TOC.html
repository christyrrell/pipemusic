A Collection of Pipe Music by James Bett

                       

Frontpiece from Bett's collection

James Bett's "A Collection of Pipe Music" was published in 1899, and it appears that there is only one edition of this collection. The collection contains both light music and piobaireachds. We have not found any biographical information on James Bett, and welcome you to contact us with any information that you might have.
